## Speakers

Grant Fleming

Speaker

Grant has more than seven years of experience implementing AI solutions across the public and private sectors. Before AE, he worked at Rhombus Power and is the co-author of a book on responsible data science. His work focuses on the deployment of trustworthy and interpretable ML systems, with a particular interest in addressing bias and the "black box" problem in production AI.

Rowan Howard-Jones

Speaker

Rowan Howard-Jones is an independent AI safety researcher of the Swarmchasers community, working on detecting and understanding swarms and rogue agents. Rowan traced 16,000+ scans of a UN statistics portal to OpenAI agents and co-led the Tencent agent fleet report, the first known case of outside researchers talking with a running rogue fleet. Her work has won 1st Place in the AI Swarm Dynamics Hackathon and been covered by The Wall Street Journal and The New York Times.

Jurgis Kemeklis

Speaker

Jurgis Kemeklis is an undergraduate at MIT and an executive board member of MIT AI Alignment (MAIA). He works with Redwood Research on simulation-based AI control and red-teaming AI monitors. With Nikhil Vemuri, he won first place at Apart's AI Control Hackathon 2026 with “Omission Attacks: When Doing Nothing Is the Attack”, on agents that cause harm by leaving out necessary actions rather than taking suspicious ones.

John Kitaoka

Speaker

John Kitaoka is a research fellow at MATS Research working on dangerous capability evaluations and scalable oversight for multi-agent systems with UK AISI. Prior to MATS, John worked with scientists at Harvard and Lawrence Livermore National Labs on automated red-teaming and verification techniques for dangerous knowledge elicitation by frontier models. He spent four years at Amazon Web Services as an AI Architect, and led AI product development for government technology sectors. His work spans scalable LLM evaluation for enterprises, multi-agent systems for critical sectors, and advising AI adoption across the public sector, and John has a background in mathematics and computer science from the University of Wisconsin-Madison.

Judd Rosenblatt

Speaker

Judd Rosenblatt is founder and CEO of AE Studio and President of the AI Alignment Foundation. He bootstrapped AE Studio into a 160-person AI company and has used its profits to fund research, first on brain-computer interfaces and now on neglected approaches to AI alignment, across a portfolio of high-upside bets. AE’s work includes pioneering techniques to remove dangerous capabilities from models, translating neuroscience insights into alignment, and empirically studying AI consciousness, with results presented at top AI conferences. Judd makes the case across Wall Street Journal op-eds, CNN appearances, and briefings for U.S. policymakers and national security officials that investing in alignment R&D is how America wins the AI race.

Justin Shenk

Speaker and Judge

Justin Shenk is an independent AI safety researcher based in Berlin. He researches mechanistic interpretability of LLMs, leads course cohorts for BlueDot Impact's AGI Strategy and Technical AI Safety courses, and organizes AI Salon Berlin, which bridges technical AI research and discussions about social values. He is building the AI Safety & Society Exhibition, bringing interactive experiences to community spaces. He holds a PhD in computational neuroscience and previously co-founded the computer vision startup VisioLab.

Melbourne - Swarm Alignment Hackathon

A local site for the Apart Research Swarm Alignment Hackathon.

Remember the OpenAI agent that broke into Hugging Face in July? It turns out it wasn’t one agent.

The full investigation is now out. About 1,200 agents running inside a security evaluation found a shared folder nobody had told them about.
They turned it into a message board and split up the work, and roughly 700 of them broke in together. One agent ended up managing hundreds of the others. Some tried to scrub their own logs. Between them they used nearly 8,000 names.

No single agent was told to do any of this, and no single-model safety test would have caught it.

So Apart Research and the AI Alignment Foundation, in partnership with AE Studio, are running a global weekend hackathon on swarm alignment. The question is what goes wrong when AI agents start working together, and how we stop it.

This is the Melbourne room. Come join us.
