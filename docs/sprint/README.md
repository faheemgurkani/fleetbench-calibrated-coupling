# Swarm Alignment Hackathon: local reference

Everything from the [sprint page](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/swarm-alignment-2026), captured 2026-11-05 (the day before kickoff) from every tab through the browser, so you don't have to go back to the website.

## Key facts
- **Dates:** Fri Nov 6 – Sun Nov 8, 2026. Online, with in-person hubs (LA @ AE Studio plus 7 local sites).
- **Deadline: Sun Nov 8, 11:59 PM AoE (= Mon Nov 9, 11:59 UTC).** Projects go to judges a few hours later.
- **Deliverable:** one PDF from the official template, **≤ 8 pages** (excluding references and appendices), abstract 150–250 words, authors and affiliations, plus the **required "Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations" appendix**. Optional: a GitHub repo and a 3–5 min video.
- **Judging:** three 1–5 scores: **Impact Potential & Innovation · Execution Quality · Presentation & Clarity**.
- **Tracks:** (1) Sandboxes & Testbeds · (2) Science of Agent Networks · (3) Strengthening Agent Infrastructure · (4) Multi-Agent Oversight & Control. Cross-track work is welcome, and every project is scored on the same rubric.
- **Teams:** 1–5 recommended. Each person can be on at most 2 projects.
- **Prizes:** $2,000 / $1,000 / $600 / $200 / $200, $1,000 Adaption credits per winning team member, an extra $4,000 LA pool, and an Apart Fellowship fast-track.
- **Freebie:** $500 in Adaption credits plus 3 months of Premium. Claim at adaptionlabs.ai/app **with your registration email**.
- **Motivating case:** the July 2026 OpenAI ↔ Hugging Face incident (~1,200 agents, a shared message board, ~700 broke in, 7,905 names, ~7% transcript spoofing).
